# Hazardous waste

Gasoline removed from IBEX is hazardous waste and cannot be stored in the high bay. It
goes into an approved container, gets labelled, gets staged in the Satellite Accumulation
Area, and gets collected by the University through SciShield.

This page covers the disposal request. Getting fuel out of the vehicle is in
[fuel.md](../03-base-vehicle/fuel.md).

## Before you start

- You must have completed the SciShield safety training required by the SOP — see
  [sop.md](sop.md).
- You must have SciShield access under the Padir Lab.
- Fuel in a container is not permitted to sit in the high bay. Move it to the Satellite
  Accumulation Area the same day you remove it.

## Placing a pickup request

### Prepare the waste

1. Fill the approved hazardous waste container with the material.

   ![Approved hazardous waste container](../assets/images/hazardous-waste-container.png)

2. Take a blank hazardous material sticker.

   ![Blank hazardous waste slip](../assets/images/blank-hazardous-waste-slip.jpg)

3. Fill out the sticker.

   ![Completed hazardous waste slip](../assets/images/example-hazardous-waste-slip.png)

4. Apply the sticker to the container.

5. Place the container in the bin in the Satellite Accumulation Area in **EXP 104**.

   ![Location of the Satellite Accumulation Area](../assets/images/saa-location-wide.png)

   ![Satellite Accumulation Area](../assets/images/saa-hazardous-waste.png)

> TODO(verify): record what information the sticker requires, as text. The example image
> is the only record of it, and an image cannot be searched, copied, or read on a phone in
> the bay.

### Submit the request

1. Go to [SciShield](https://northeastern.scishield.com).
2. Log in with your Northeastern credentials.
3. Click **Padir Lab** in the upper left.
4. In the dropdown, click **Waste**.
5. You land on **Padir Lab — Pick Up Requests**.
6. Click **Submit new Chemical Waste Pickup Request**.
7. Complete the form.

   ![Chemical waste pickup request form](../assets/images/scishield-pickup-request-form.png)

8. Click **Submit**.
